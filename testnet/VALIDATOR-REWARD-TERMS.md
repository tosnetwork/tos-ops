# Validator Reward Terms and Eligibility Policy

**Public testnet validator campaign, December 5–19, 2026.**

**Status: draft, under legal review.** This policy is the single set of rules for every participant, in every country. It will be finalised and published before testing begins on December 5, 2026. Until then, any part of it may change. No conclusion has yet been reached that the campaign, the reward or the TOS token complies with the law of any particular jurisdiction. The following matters are still under review:

- the legal classification of the TOS token;
- the structure of the reward source;
- the entity that makes the payment;
- the services agreement between that entity and each participant.

If that review requires it, the team may change the eligibility, documentation or payment terms for any jurisdiction, and will announce the change.

**This policy is not legal, tax or financial advice.** Ask your own advisers about your situation.

The campaign itself is described in the [validator invitation](ANNOUNCEMENT-20261205.md). If the two documents differ on a reward, eligibility or tax question, this policy prevails.

## 1. Definitions

- **Campaign:** the public testnet validator campaign described in the invitation.
- **Participant:** an individual or entity whose registration the coordinator has accepted.
- **Operator:** the person who actually controls a validator host and its consensus key. One operator is one participant, however many hosts, wallets, pools or validators they run.
- **Paying entity:** the legal entity that will pay the reward and sign the services terms with each participant. It will be named in the final version of this policy.
- **Reward:** 1,000 mainnet TOS per qualified participant, on the conditions below.

## 2. What the reward is, and what it is not

**What it is.**
- The reward is compensation for technical testing services: operating a validator on the public testnet, running the requested tests, and reporting verified results and feedback.
- It is earned only by doing that work and having it verified.

**What it is not.**
- It is not offered as an investment, a sale of tokens, a share in any enterprise, or a promise of profit.
- No purchase, deposit, payment or contribution of any kind is required to take part or to qualify.
- The team makes no statement about the present or future price, market or liquidity of TOS. A TOS reward may have no market value when it is paid, or afterwards.

**Where it comes from.**
- The reward is funded from the staking rewards that validators operated by the TOS team earn on mainnet.
- It is not new issuance or an allocation from the genesis supply.
- It is paid only as those rewards accrue. Payment can therefore start only after mainnet launches, and may come in instalments.
- Mainnet has no fixed launch date. The reward is not owed if mainnet never launches.

**Limits.**
- At most 21 participants can receive the reward in this campaign. If more than 21 qualify, the selection rule published before December 5 applies.
- A participant can receive the reward only once, however many validators they operate.

## 3. Eligibility

You may take part, and may receive the reward, only if all of the following are true:

1. You are an individual who has reached the age of majority where you live, and at least 18 years old, or an entity acting through an authorised representative.
2. You can enter into a binding agreement under the law that applies to you.
3. You are not, and you are not owned or controlled by:
   - a person on a sanctions list maintained by the United Nations, the United States (including the U.S. Treasury's OFAC lists), the European Union or the United Kingdom; or
   - a person ordinarily resident in, or organised under the law of, a country or region subject to comprehensive sanctions under those regimes.
4. Taking part, and receiving and holding the reward, are lawful for you where you live and where you are a tax resident.
5. You are not a member, employee or contractor of the TOS team, the paying entity or any coordinator of this campaign. Nor are you participating on behalf of one of them, or registering an identity for someone else.
6. You meet the identity and tax documentation requirements in section 4 before payment.

**Residents of the United States are not excluded.** U.S. persons may register and take part. To receive the reward, they must meet every condition in this policy, including the U.S. tax documentation in section 4.

The paying entity may refuse a registration or a payment, or delay a payment, where the law or a sanctions rule requires it, or where a participant does not meet this section. It will give the reason unless the law forbids it.

## 4. Identity, sanctions screening and tax documentation

Before any reward is paid, each qualified participant must complete the steps below. They are collected only after mainnet launch, through a channel the team announces. They are never collected before then, and never through a direct message.

1. **Identity verification.**
   - Verify your identity with the verification provider the paying entity names.
   - This is how one-reward-per-person is enforced, and how sanctions screening is done.
   - Only identity documents go to the provider. Never send a private key, mnemonic, consensus seed or vault credential to anyone.
2. **Sanctions screening.** Your identity and your payment address are screened against the lists in section 3. A participant or address that fails screening is not paid.
3. **Tax documentation.**
   - **U.S. persons** (U.S. citizens, U.S. tax residents and U.S. entities) provide IRS Form W-9, with a taxpayer identification number.
   - **Everyone else** provides IRS Form W-8BEN (individuals) or W-8BEN-E (entities), or the equivalent form the paying entity requests under the law that applies to it.
   - A participant who does not provide valid tax documentation is not paid.
4. **Reporting and withholding.**
   - The paying entity may be required to report payments to tax authorities. For U.S. persons this may include an information return in the IRS Form 1099 series.
   - Where the law requires, it may withhold tax from the reward, for example backup withholding when a U.S. person has not provided a valid taxpayer identification number.
   - Any amount withheld is reported as the law requires.
   - The paying entity does not increase the reward to cover tax.
5. **Payment address.** You provide a mainnet address that you control. A payment sent to the address you gave is final. The team cannot recover tokens sent to a wrong or lost address.

## 5. Taxes

**You alone are responsible for the taxes on the reward.** That includes income tax, social contributions, value added or sales tax, and any tax due when you later sell, exchange or dispose of the reward. Ask your own tax adviser.

**General information, not advice.**
- Many tax authorities treat cryptocurrency received as payment for services as income, valued at its fair market value when it is received.
- In the United States, IRS guidance (Notice 2014-21 and later guidance on digital assets) treats virtual currency received as payment for services as income at its fair market value when it is received. U.S. federal income tax returns also ask whether the taxpayer received digital assets as payment for services.
- Your own treatment depends on your circumstances and the law that applies to you.

The team does not give tax advice and makes no representation about how the reward will be taxed in your case.

## 6. Payment terms

1. **When.** The reward is paid only after mainnet launches and only as the team-operated validators' rewards accrue, possibly in instalments. No payment date is guaranteed.
2. **Claim window.** Claim instructions are published after mainnet launch. They include a claim window of at least 90 days. A participant who does not complete section 4 within the window forfeits the reward. The team announces each window in the official channels.
3. **Form.** The reward is paid only in mainnet TOS, never in testnet tokens and never in cash or any other asset.
4. **No transfer of the claim.** The right to a pending reward is personal. It cannot be sold, assigned, pledged or transferred.
5. **Errors.** If a payment is made in error, or in excess of what this policy allows, the participant must return the excess.

## 7. Conduct and disqualification

The paying entity may disqualify a participant, withhold or reclaim a reward, and exclude them from future campaigns, if the participant:

- registers more than one identity, or registers for another person (Sybil or duplicate participation);
- colludes with other participants, or fabricates or alters test evidence, logs or results;
- attacks, disrupts or exploits the testnet, other participants' hosts or the team's infrastructure. Security issues must be reported privately to the team, as described in the repository's security policy;
- asks for, shares or uses anyone else's keys or credentials;
- gives false information under sections 3 or 4; or
- breaks the law or this policy in connection with the campaign.

## 8. Changes and cancellation

- **Before December 5, 2026:** the team may change or cancel the campaign and this policy by announcing it in the official channels.
- **After December 5, 2026:** the published rules change only to correct errors, to follow the law, or as the legal review described at the top of this policy requires. Every change is announced.
- If the campaign does not proceed, for example because fewer than five qualified external applicants register by the deadline, no reward is owed under it.

## 9. Personal data

- **What is collected for the test:** your operator name, contact handle, time zone, VPS public IP address and the public records you send.
- **What is collected for payment:** identity and tax documentation, collected by the verification provider and the paying entity.
- **What it is used for:** running the test, verifying results, enforcing one reward per person, sanctions screening, tax compliance, and paying rewards. Nothing else.
- **How long it is kept:**
  - Test data is kept for the campaign and deleted on request afterwards.
  - Identity and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires.
- **Public chain data** stays public.
- **Your rights:** where a data protection law gives you rights, such as access, correction or deletion, ask the coordinator to exercise them.

## 10. Risk and liability

**Testnet risk.**
- The testnet is experimental and provided as is.
- It may be paused, reset or ended at any time.
- Testnet TOS has no value.

**Your costs and your keys.**
- Participants run their own hosts and bear their own costs, including VPS, networking and electricity.
- Participants alone are responsible for the security of their keys.
- The team never accesses participants' hosts.

**Limitation of liability.** To the extent the law allows, neither the team nor the paying entity is liable for indirect or consequential losses arising from the campaign. Their total liability to any participant is limited to the reward that participant has qualified for.
